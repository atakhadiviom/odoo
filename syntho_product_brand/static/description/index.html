Catalog Structure

Syntho Product Brand

Add a dedicated brand master to Odoo 19 and link it directly to product templates for cleaner catalog organization.

Odoo 19 Product Brand Master Sales Ready

Core Features

Everything needed to introduce brands without overcomplicating the product model.

Dedicated Brand Model

Create brand records with a name, description, logo, and related products.

Brand on Product Form

A brand field is added to the product template form right next to core catalog fields.

Readable Catalog Data

Give sales and inventory teams a cleaner way to capture brand information at source.

Controlled Access

Internal users can read brands, while system administrators manage the master data.

Where It Fits

  • Businesses that sell multiple brands and want that relationship visible on products.
  • Teams preparing cleaner catalogs before building custom reports, filters, or portals.
  • Odoo instances where brand should be part of the core product record rather than free text.

Data Model

The addon introduces a new product.brand model and links it to product.template through a many-to-one brand field.

Installation

Step 1

Copy syntho_product_brand into your addons path.

Step 2

Restart Odoo and update the Apps list.

Step 3

Install Syntho Product Brand.

Step 4

Create brand records and assign them on product templates.

Technical Snapshot

Dependencies product, sale
New Model product.brand
Brand Fields Name, description, logo, and related products
Access Rules Read for internal users, full management for system administrators
