Catalog Structure

Syntho Product Brand

Add a dedicated brand master to your Odoo product catalog and assign brands directly on product templates with a clean, business-friendly management screen.

Odoo 19 Product Catalog Sales Brand Master Data

Why Teams Use It

Odoo's standard product model does not provide a simple brand entity for many businesses that sell third-party or multi-brand catalogs. This module introduces a dedicated product.brand model and links it to the product template form so brand information becomes a first-class catalog field.

Dedicated Brand Records

Create and manage brand entries with a name, description, and logo.

Product Template Integration

Assign a brand directly on the product form without changing your existing product structure.

Clean Admin Navigation

Brand records are available from the Sales catalog menu for easy maintenance.

Access Control Included

Internal users can read brand data while administrators manage brand records.

Good fit for:
  • trading companies and distributors with multi-brand catalogs
  • manufacturers that want consistent brand tagging across product templates
  • teams preparing exports, reports, or automation flows that depend on brand data

Installation

1
Copy the module to your addons path Place syntho_product_brand inside the custom addons directory used by your Odoo instance.
2
Refresh the Apps list Update the app registry so Odoo can detect the module.
3
Install Syntho Product Brand Install the add-on from Apps and open the Product Brands menu.
4
Create brands and assign them to products Maintain brand records from Sales, then select the brand on each product template.
Dependencies product, sale
Main Fields Brand name, description, logo, and product template link
Technical Scope One new brand model plus a simple extension of the product template form
