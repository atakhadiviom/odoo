Visual Documents

Product Images on Reports

Add product thumbnails to quotations, sales orders, invoices, and purchase orders so printed documents are easier to verify at a glance.

Odoo 19 Sales Accounting Purchase

Document Coverage

The module enhances standard PDF reports without adding new business objects.

Quotations and Sales Orders

Show item thumbnails on sales documents for clearer customer-facing proposals.

Customer Invoices

Help customers match billed products visually and reduce confusion on final invoices.

Purchase Orders

Give buyers and vendors a faster visual check when ordering similar-looking products.

Graceful Fallback

If a product has no image, the report still renders cleanly without broken placeholders.

Implementation Approach

The addon uses QWeb template inheritance and report styling only. There are no new models, no business logic changes, and no extra settings screen to maintain.

Best Fit

  • Product catalogs with many similar items
  • Teams sending branded, visual quotations
  • Companies wanting clearer vendor and customer PDFs

Installation

Step 1

Copy report_product_image into your addons path.

Step 2

Restart Odoo and update the Apps list.

Step 3

Install Product Images on Reports.

Step 4

Print a quotation, invoice, or purchase order and the thumbnails appear automatically.
