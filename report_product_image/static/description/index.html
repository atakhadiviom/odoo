Product Images on Reports

Add clean product thumbnails to quotations, invoices, and purchase orders so customers, buyers, and warehouse teams can identify the right items at a glance.

Odoo 19 Sales Accounting Purchase

Designed for Better Documents

This module extends Odoo's standard QWeb report templates and inserts a dedicated image column before the product description. It keeps the layout clean, leaves section and note rows aligned, and shows nothing when a product has no thumbnail available.

Sales Orders and Quotations

Show product visuals directly on customer-facing proposals and confirmations.

Customer Invoices

Help finance teams and customers cross-check what was billed more quickly.

Purchase Orders

Give suppliers and receiving teams a clearer reference for ordered products.

Lightweight by Design

No extra models or backend configuration - only report template inheritance and CSS.

Business Value

Useful When

  • your products are easier to identify visually than by SKU alone
  • you send quotations for furniture, retail, trading, or catalog-heavy items
  • buyers and vendors need a quick visual check before approving a document

How It Behaves

  • uses the product thumbnail image for better PDF performance
  • keeps report rows aligned for products, sections, and note lines
  • falls back cleanly when no product image is stored

Installation

1
Copy the add-on to your custom addons path Place report_product_image in the directory your Odoo server reads for custom modules.
2
Refresh the Apps list Update the app registry so Odoo recognizes the module.
3
Install Product Images on Reports Install from the Apps menu like any other Odoo add-on.
4
Print your documents Quotation, invoice, and purchase order PDFs will automatically include the image column.
Dependencies sale, account, purchase
