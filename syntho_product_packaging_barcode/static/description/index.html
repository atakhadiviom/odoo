Product Packaging Barcode

Odoo 19 Inventory Community Edition

In Odoo 19, the Packaging / Barcode section is no longer visible in the Inventory tab by default. This module restores the visibility of the packaging section in the product Inventory tab, allowing users to assign multiple barcodes to the same product (including box/carton barcodes).

Features

Packaging Tab Restored

Restores the Packaging/Barcode section in the product Inventory tab to Odoo 19.

Multiple Barcodes per Product

Easily manage and assign multiple barcodes (like box, carton, or pallet barcodes) to the same product directly from the product form.

Seamless Integration

Uses standard Odoo XML view inheritance to cleanly add the Packaging field without breaking existing views or logic.

How to Use

1
Install the Product Packaging Barcode module from your Apps list.
2
Navigate to Inventory > Products > Products and open any product.
3
Go to the Inventory tab of the product form.
4
Scroll down to the Packaging section to add your packaging types, quantities, and their respective barcodes.
