Inventory and Barcodes

Syntho Product Packaging Barcode

Restore a practical Packaging / Barcodes block on the product Inventory tab in Odoo 19 so teams can maintain packaging-related barcodes directly from the product screen.

Odoo 19 Inventory Single Variant Flow Barcode Entry

What It Restores

Odoo 19 changed how packaging barcode data is handled. This add-on brings back an editable Packaging / Barcodes block inside the Inventory tab for single-variant products, making the barcode workflow easier to reach from the product form.

Inline Barcode Maintenance

Add or edit packaging barcode rows directly inside the product form.

Built on Odoo 19 Data Models

Uses Odoo 19's packaging barcode relation instead of introducing new custom tables.

Cleaner Inventory Workflow

Warehouse or operations users can maintain barcode-related packaging data without leaving the product record.

Minimal Functional Scope

The module focuses on restoring a useful entry point in the standard UI rather than replacing Odoo's barcode engine.

Important behavior:
  • the block is shown for single-variant products
  • multi-variant products still follow Odoo's variant-based workflow
  • the add-on depends on the standard inventory and barcode behavior already present in Odoo

Installation

1
Copy the add-on to your custom addons path Place syntho_product_packaging_barcode in your Odoo custom addons directory.
2
Update the Apps list Refresh Odoo's app list so the new module is visible for installation.
3
Install the module Search for Syntho Product Packaging Barcode and install it.
4
Open a product and go to Inventory For single-variant products, the Packaging / Barcodes section appears inside the Inventory tab.
