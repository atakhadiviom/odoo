Unique Product Internal Reference

A simple module to enforce uniqueness for the Internal Reference (SKU) field across all Product Templates and Variants in Odoo.

Key Features

  • Enforces strict uniqueness for the 'default_code' field.
  • Prevents identical SKUs on Product Templates.
  • Prevents identical SKUs on Product Variants.
  • Gracefully allows empty or blank references.

Use Cases

This module answers a common need among Odoo users who want to avoid accidental duplications in their product catalogs.

Maintain a clean and accurate SKU list automatically, ensuring smoother warehouse and sales operations.
